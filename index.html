About

Welcome to ICICS 2027

The 29th International Conference on Information and Communications Security (ICICS 2027) will be held in Chengdu, China.

Established in 1997, ICICS is a long-running international forum for researchers and practitioners to present and discuss advances in information and communications security. ICICS 2027 continues this tradition with a focused technical programme and peer-reviewed research contributions.

Hosted by University of Electronic Science and Technology of China

For Authors

Submission Guide

Preliminary information. The official ICICS 2027 author instructions, page limits, review rules and submission link have not yet been released. The points below summarize the ICICS 2026 rules for reference and may change for 2027.
  • Papers were required to be original, unpublished work and not under review elsewhere.
  • Submissions were anonymous and prepared in English for double-blind review.
  • Only PDF files with embedded fonts in Springer LNCS format were accepted.
  • ICICS 2026 limited papers to 18 pages including the bibliography, excluding well-marked appendices, with no more than 20 pages in total.
  • At least one author of each accepted paper had to register at the full rate and present the paper.
